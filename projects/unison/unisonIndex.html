Frequently Asked Questions

What is TuneUp! ?

TuneUp! is a music-based app that helps you use music more intentionally throughout your day. Instead of relying on a classic alarm or endless snoozing, TuneUp! lets you wake up with your favorite songs, helping you start the day in a more natural and pleasant way. You can also choose to receive optional reminders for when it’s time to go to bed, fall asleep with a preset sleep timer, or enter focus and relax modes using timed music sessions designed to match your moment. TuneUp! works seamlessly with Apple Music, so you can use the songs and playlists you already love, without managing yet another music library.

Does TuneUp! require Apple Music Subscription?

TuneUp! requires access to the Apple Music library, but an active subscription is not strictly required. If you have an active Apple Music subscription, you can use your personal playlists, albums, and saved music directly inside TuneUp!. If you don’t have a subscription, you can still use TuneUp! with Apple Music’s free radio stations. TuneUp! adapts to your Apple Music access level, letting you use music in a more intentional way whether you’re subscribed or not.

Does TuneUp! require authentication?

No. TuneUp! does not require you to create an account or sign in. The app only asks for permission to access your Apple Music account in order to play music and use Apple Music features. No additional authentication or user profile is required to use TuneUp!

Does TuneUp! collect or share personal data?

No. TuneUp! does not collect, store, or share personal data. The app does not create user profiles, track listening behavior, or distribute data to third parties. Any preferences you set — such as timers, modes, or notification settings — are stored locally on your device. Access to Apple Music is handled entirely through Apple’s official frameworks, following Apple’s privacy standards.

Does TuneUp! work offline?

Yes — as long as the music is available in your Apple Music library. TuneUp! does not stream content on its own. It only accesses the music already present in your Apple Music library, including songs, albums, and playlists you’ve added or downloaded for offline use. If that content is available offline, TuneUp! will work without an internet connection. Availability depends on how your Apple Music library is set up and whether the content has been downloaded to your device.

Can I use my own playlists with TuneUp?

Yes. TuneUp! is designed to work with your own Apple Music library. If you have an active Apple Music subscription, you can use your personal playlists, albums, and saved tracks for alarms, sleep timers, focus sessions, and relaxation modes. TuneUp! simply lets you reuse the music you already love, in a more intentional way.

Does TuneUp support multiple languages?

Yes. TuneUp! supports multiple languages, including: English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Indonesian, and Korean. More languages will be added over time.
