Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Vision Note doesn't collect your data. Everything you write stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The goals you write in Vision Note are stored locally on your device. That data never reaches us — we have no servers, no backend, and no way to access what you write.

In-App Purchases (Tips)

If you choose to leave a tip, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment information. Apple handles it completely — their privacy practices are described at apple.com/privacy.

Analytics and tracking

None. Vision Note uses no analytics tools, crash reporters, or third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests.

Third-party sharing

We don't share your data with anyone — because we don't have your data.

Children's privacy

Vision Note does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and others)

If you're in the EU, California, or another region with privacy rights laws: since we collect no personal data, those rights apply to data stored on your own device, which you control directly. There is nothing on our end to access, correct, delete, or export.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
